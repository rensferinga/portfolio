Spruit is a digital app for phones and tablets, designed to support young people dealing with mental health challenges. The app offers a personalised journey through tailored conversations, a structured roadmap and interactive mini-games based on each user's needs. A customisable mascot helps build trust and creates a more personal connection throughout the experience.
