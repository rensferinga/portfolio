Horror Stories is an episodic horror series featuring unique stories and atmospheres. The first episode is a psychological horror experience set in the snowy villages and mountains of Norway. You play as a train conductor, operating your own train while clearing snow-covered tracks, adjusting railway switches and keeping the railway clear during a heavy snowstorm.
