Verbeelding is a short animated film created for a school project. It follows a boy who gets lost in his own imagination, with a paper airplane returning throughout the story. The film combines a 3D environment made in Blender with hand-drawn 2D animation using Grease Pencil. I created the house, while some background elements, such as the clouds, were not drawn by me.

  • Concept, drawings and story
  • 3D modelling and animation
  • 2D animation with Grease Pencil
