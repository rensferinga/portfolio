Eemsdelta is an interactive installation developed to encourage people to explore the Eemsdelta region. Displayed on a large screen at the Forum in Groningen, visitors create a personalised avatar through a questionnaire. Their avatar explores a virtual island inspired by the region and its points of interest, discovering the history of local buildings and landmarks.

  • Development of the interactive installation
  • Connecting the physical and online experience
  • Lead developer

Klanktoon is an interactive installation designed to show how accessible making music can be. By moving your hands in front of a screen, you can create music through colours and sounds. Each finger produces a different note, and you can switch between various musical styles to create your own compositions.

Besides my main projects, I enjoy experimenting with different ideas, games and interactive experiences.

Matcha Café Simulator is a cozy café simulator prototype, created as a concept for a café in Groningen. Custom 3D assets and gameplay logic bring the experience to life.

No Man's Area is an experimental semi-open-world looter shooter set in Japan, featuring Japanese-inspired architecture and environments. The project is also a personal learning experience focused on Blender, Unreal Engine and game development.
