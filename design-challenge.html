Design Challenge was an international design competition where I was selected, together with a small group of classmates, to represent our school at ICDC in Antwerp. Within one week, we developed a design solution for Tomorrowland's waste problem, where festival visitors often leave their rubbish behind. We created Metamorphosis, a concept focused on bringing people together through a fun, shared experience that encourages more responsible waste disposal. I also created the branding for our team, including the logo, and made the AI-generated video visuals, such as the cocoon. The other AI-generated still images were not created by me. I created the full PowerPoint presentation as well.
