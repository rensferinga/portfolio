Glas te Ver is a short VR experience developed for a school project, exploring the dangers of drunk driving. Accompanied by a companion, you make choices about how much you drink before getting behind the wheel. You then take control of a virtual car, experiencing how alcohol gradually affects your vision, reaction time and ability to stay in control of the road.
